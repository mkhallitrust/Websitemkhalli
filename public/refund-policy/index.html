Seva bookings

  • Cancellation by you: email mkhallitrust@gmail.com or call +91 94480 76834 with your Order ID at least 3 days before the seva date for a full refund. Later cancellations cannot be refunded, because preparations for the seva will have begun, but we can move the seva to another date once.
  • Cancellation by the Trust: if a seva cannot be performed on the booked date, you may choose another date or a full refund.

Donations

Donations are not refundable once received, except when a payment was made by mistake, twice, or for a wrong amount. Write to us within 7 days with the Order ID and we will refund the extra amount.

Failed or pending payments

If money is deducted but the payment page shows failed or pending, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank within 5–7 working days. If it is not, contact us with the Order ID.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Cashfree Payments within 5–7 working days of approval. We do not pay refunds in cash.
